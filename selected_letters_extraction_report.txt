KEY LETTER CONNECTIONS & THEMES
================================================================================

1. CANADIAN PERIOD (Vancouver, X-Kalay)
   34 letters discuss this critical 1972 period
   Setting: Guest of Honor at Vancouver SF convention, heroin rehabilitation at X-Kalay Foundation
   Key works discussed: Flow My Tears, Scanner Darkly
   Themes: Suicide attempt recovery, spiritual rebirth, authenticity struggle

2. MAJOR WORKS DEVELOPMENT

   Flow My Tears, the Policeman Said (38 letters):
   - Central work during extraction period
   - Discussed contextually within financial crisis, recovery, and intellectual development
   - Associated with comeback from two-year writing hiatus

   A Scanner Darkly (22 letters):
   - Emerging work under discussion
   - Heavily informed by heroin addiction observations at X-Kalay
   - Explores themes of authenticity, identity, and controlled substances
   - Shows PKD processing personal crisis through fiction

   Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? (18 letters):
   - Earlier work discussed in context of movie options
   - Part of ongoing conversation about simulacra theme
   - Connected to philosophical arguments about authenticity

   The Man in the High Castle (18 letters):
   - Hugo Award-winning work frequently referenced
   - Discussed in context of reprint rights, sales, recognition
   - Anchor work for PKD's career identity

3. INTELLECTUAL EVOLUTION
   Letters show clear progression from personal crisis to philosophical grounding:
   - March 1972: Raw pain, loneliness, despair from Vancouver period
   - April-May 1972: X-Kalay rehabilitation, observation of heroin addiction
   - Summer-Fall 1972: Integration of experience into philosophical framework
   - 1973: Full development of simulacra/authenticity philosophy

   Key insight: Heroin addiction becomes metaphor for existential death (Thanos vs Eros)
   Core evolution: Understanding addiction as severing from Being, choosing Nothingness

4. BIOGRAPHICAL CONTINUITY
   72 letters reference family/children - Christopher's birth (July 1973) major milestone
   26 letters discuss marriage milestones - remarriage to Tessa Busby
   9 letters reference suicide crisis - survival and recovery narrative arc

   Pattern: Personal crisis and recovery cycles inform all intellectual work
