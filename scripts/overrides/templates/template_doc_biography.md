# Template — Biography / Memoir / Psychobiography Summary

For: book-length biographical works, ex-spouse memoirs, psychobiographical studies.
Lane: **D** (Synthesis) for biographies; **E** (Primary) for first-person memoirs by people who knew PKD.

## card_summary
**Length:** 70–100 words.
**Required structure:**
1. Form (full biography / memoir / psychobiography) and author's relationship to PKD.
2. Coverage — life-span treated, sources used, methodological frame.
3. Position relative to other biographies — first / standard / corrective / supplementary.

## page_summary
**Length:** 300–700 words.

**Required sections (as paragraphs):**

1. **Bibliographic identity** — author, year, publisher, length; for memoirs, the author's specific tie to PKD with dates.
2. **Coverage and method** — what life-period it treats; what sources it draws on (interviews, letters, archive holdings, the Exegesis, the author's own memory); where it concentrates and where it skims.
3. **Original material** — what this biography contributes that no prior account has: previously unpublished letters, fresh interviews, archive access, witness testimony.
4. **Interpretive frame** — the reading of PKD this biographer offers (mystic, schizophrenic, postmodern artist, working-class intellectual, gnostic visionary, drug casualty, etc.). Attribute as the biographer's reading, not as fact.
5. **Contradictions with other biographies** — explicit. Where does this account disagree with Sutin, Anne Dick, Rickman, Arnold, Peake, Tessa Dick? Per ontology §4, name the disputed events.
6. **Reception** — how scholars and fans have used it; known errors corrected in later editions; reviews.

If the biography discusses universities, lecture sites, conferences, residences, archives, or other exact or approximate locations, make those places visible in the biography timeline and the map catalog.

## Required JSON fields
- All scholarship fields, plus:
- `evidentiary_lane` — `D` for biographies, `E` for primary-witness memoirs
- `source_reliability` — `biography` or `primary_witness`

- [ ] States the biographer's relationship to PKD.

- [ ] Surfaces at least two contradictions with other accounts.
- [ ] Cross-links populated, including links to the other biographies it agrees or disagrees with.
