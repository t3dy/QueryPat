# Template — Interview / Interview Compilation Summary

For: single interviews, edited interview compilations, oral history transcripts.
Lane: **E** (Primary).

## card_summary
**Length:** 60–90 words.

**Required structure:**
1. Who interviewed PKD, when, in what venue.
2. The occasion (book release, convention, posthumous compilation, late-life retrospective).
3. What the interview is most cited for — the topics PKD discussed at length.

## page_summary
**Length:** 250–500 words.

**Required sections:**

1. **Bibliographic identity** — interviewer, date of interview (distinct from publication date), publication venue, length, format (single sitting / multi-session / compilation).
2. **Occasion and tone** — what prompted the interview, what mood PKD was in (where recoverable), how candid he is.
3. **Topics covered** — the actual ground. Name them: 2-3-74, the early novels, the drug period, the FBI letters, the Vancouver speech, the Bishop Pike events, *Blade Runner*, the Exegesis, particular works.
4. **Notable claims and quotes** — the lines later scholars cite. PKD's claims here are *self-reports*; record them as such.
5. **Reliability flag** — PKD revised his self-presentation across interviews. If this interview's account of an event contradicts other interviews or biographies, surface the contradiction.
6. **Cross-references** — linked terms, named people, works discussed, biography events corroborated or contradicted.

If the interview mentions a university, lecture venue, conference, archive, or other locatable institutional site, surface it for the biography timeline and the map catalog as well as the interview summary.

## Required JSON fields
- `evidentiary_lane` — `E`
- `source_reliability` — `primary_self_report`

## Editorial caution
Interviews are PKD's self-reports. Per ontology §5, claims made here are not facts about events; they are facts *about what PKD said in this interview about events*. The summary must not collapse the distinction.

## Lint checklist
- [ ] Distinguishes interview date from publication date.
- [ ] Names the topics covered.
- [ ] Treats PKD's claims as self-reports, not as confirmed events.
- [ ] Cross-links populated.
