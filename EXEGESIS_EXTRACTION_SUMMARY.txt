MAJOR THEOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENTS:
================================

1. TIME AS FORCE/CONSCIOUSNESS
   PKD argues time is not subjective perception but an active energy field.
   When time weakens, reality's underlying substrate appears - this is the Ubik experience.
   Holy Spirit moves retrograde in time while Logos operates outside it.

2. FUTURE AS INFORMANT
   Information constantly leaks backward from the future (tachyonic transmission).
   This appears as: dreams, visions, prophecy, instinct, synchronicity.
   Humans receive future knowledge "naturally" but misinterpret it as divine revelation
   or subconsciousness rather than recognizing it as temporal information flow.

3. REALITY LAYERS/ONTOLOGY
   This world is false/inferior (often called Black Iron Prison).
   A transcendent real world exists behind appearances.
   Consciousness is imprisoned in lower material reality by a false god or system.
   This maps onto: Gnosticism, Platonism, Hindu Maya/Brahman, Buddhist Samsara.

4. DIVINE MANIFESTATION
   God/Logos appeared to PKD as pink/gold luminous entity (March 1974).
   Also manifests through: cat healing, dreams, voice on radio, prophetic knowledge.
   Is both personal (entering PKD) and impersonal (universal consciousness).
   Characterized as Holy Spirit, Logos, Ubik, Zebra, Plasmate, VALIS.

5. CONSCIOUSNESS SURVIVAL/CONTINUITY
   Dead can "come through" (Jim Pike example).
   Identity may be transferable between bodies/times.
   Consciousness is not generated by brain but flows through it.
   Personal immortality is possible through maintaining consciousness identity.

6. GOOD/EVIL COSMIC PRINCIPLE
   The Lie vs. The Truth as fundamental cosmic forces.
   Zoroastrian dualism: Ahura Mazda (Light) vs. Angra Mainyu (Darkness).
   Evil is not mere absence but active anti-reality principle.
   Human liberation requires recognizing The Lie and rejecting it.

CONNECTIONS TO NOVELS:
======================

Ubik (1969):
  - Central exploration of reality dissolution
  - Characters dead but don't know it - reality is subjective projection
  - Ubik force/spray: entropy reversal, communication from future/transcendent
  - Presciently depicts Exegesis themes before 1974 vision

VALIS (1981):
  - Direct fictionalization of 2-3-74 events
  - Zebra (plasmate entity) = the pink/gold being PKD experienced
  - Black Iron Prison = world of deception
  - Divine Intervention into history through plasmate contact

The Divine Invasion (1981):
  - Continuation of VALIS themes
  - God incarnate returns in present time
  - Gnosticism explicitly dramatized
  - Reality layers and false world clearly depicted

A Scanner Darkly (1977):
  - Paranoia about surveillance/manipulation
  - Identity dissolution and schizophrenia
  - Drug use (Substance D) as consciousness alteration
  - Question of what is real vs. observed

Flow My Tears, The Policeman Said (1974):
  - Pre-vision novel treating themes that emerged afterward
  - Reality instability and identity loss
  - Actor loses his identity/existence

INTERNAL CONTRADICTIONS NOTED:
==============================

1. CAUSATION PARADOX
   Are the novels causing the world to become PKD-like?
   Or are both novels and reality being written by future Ubik/Logos?
   Or is it all memory: retrieving facts already determined?

2. DEATH VS. SURVIVAL
   Is PKD himself dead (Ubik scenario)?
   Is consciousness Jim Pike using PKD's body?
   Or is PKD a recovered pre-1964 personality after amnesia?
   Or has he achieved temporal simultaneity across multiple times?

3. ILLUSION VS. HIGHER REALITY
   Is this world absolutely false (Gnostic view)?
   Or is it lower but real, with transcendent reality beyond?
   Or are multiple equally-real layers existing simultaneously?

4. DIVINE NATURE
   Is the Being he contacted (Zebra/Ubik/Logos) God?
   Or an advanced intelligence/future human consciousness?
   Or multiple separate entities appearing as one?
   Single Logos vs. plural Logoi - are they different beings?

5. KNOWLEDGE SOURCE
   Are visions/dreams genuine communications from transcendent reality?
   Or manifestations of PKD's own unconscious/right-brain?
   Or both simultaneously (consciousness is unified across time)?
