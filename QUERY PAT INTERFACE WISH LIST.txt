The Three Features That Will Transform QueryPat

If you only implemented three things from this document, they should be:

1. Cross-site tag pages

They turn the site into a conceptual explorer.

2. Grouped search results

They make the archive navigable.

3. Backlinks

They reveal hidden connections automatically.
