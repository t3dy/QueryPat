## **141**

There are many wonderful literatures that can serve as chronicles of this death foretold by Zarathustra: Joyce, Eliot, Woolf, Barnes, Ellison, Burroughs —take your pick. And Dick has likewise oft been included in such company. As Fredric Jameson puts it, Dick’s is “a literature of the so-called ‘death of the subject,’ of an end to individualism so absolute as to call into question the last glimmers of the ego” (347).
