# Memory & Identity

Memory and identity are fused in Dick's work, and both can fail, fragment, or be rewritten under pressure.

Amnesia, implanted or false memory, identity instability, and the porous self.

This page reads the fiction corpus through that lens, with each work linked back to its canonical record.

## Reading the works

### Clans of the Alphane Moon

Clans of the Alphane Moon is the canonical works record for PKD's novel. In a novel like [Clans of the Alphane Moon](/works/clans-of-the-alphane-moon), that premise becomes a thematic pressure point instead of background decoration. Recalling, forgetting, and misremembering become plot devices that make the self feel provisional. The theme emerges through reversals, doubling, and the small formal choices that make Dick's fiction feel unstable from inside.

## Synthesis

Across these works, memory & identity is not a static topic but a literary machine: Dick uses it to strain plot logic, expose false surfaces, and test whether a character's inner life can survive contact with the system around them. The fiction does not simply mention the theme; it stages it as a recurring formal event.
